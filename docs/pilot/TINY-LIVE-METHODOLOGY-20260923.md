## Evidence retention

The shared streaming recorder now writes raw monitoring into an eight-segment ring, each at most 8 MiB. Ordinary raw traffic may continue beyond 256 MiB; total retained raw is at most 64 MiB. Each sealed segment has counts, sequence bounds and a SHA-256 digest. Before evicting monitoring segments, a durable manifest records their aggregate count/bytes and cumulative digest. Evicted raw monitoring cannot be replayed and is explicitly described as unavailable. Full-session compact counters/extrema remain in summaries, so reports do not silently treat the retained tail as the whole run.

Decision evidence uses a separate append-only, fsynced hash chain with a 64 MiB hard budget. Admission/confirmation context includes the exact pair, review, quantity, quote, books and feed health; the request and complete response proof are retained. Stream records during confirmation are protected too, including failure or deadline outcomes. The live ledger independently preserves frozen plans, intent before submission, actual reply/outcome, reconciled fill evidence and recovery decisions; it never reads a PAPER database. An attempted strategy trade must never rely solely on an evictable raw segment.

Per-record maximum is 2 MiB, process RSS limit 768 MiB, and minimum disk headroom 2 GiB. Protected-budget exhaustion, oversized records, short writes, sync errors, disk reserve or memory exhaustion latch the writer closed and stop admission/monitoring. Nothing is silently dropped to keep a strategy running. Protected evidence requires deliberate reviewed archival before its finite budget is exhausted; unlimited trade history on finite disk is not claimed. Used evidence directories cannot be automatically reused after a crash. Raw crash tails may be incomplete; critical writes must be durable before proceeding. The legacy bounded study keeps its separate interval/statistical resource limits; the new monitor uses compact per-route counters with bounded confirmation/event lists.

An offline stress test writes over 256 MiB of explicitly synthetic monitoring data, forces multiple rotations, checks retained disk bounds and verifies the protected hash chain after rotation. Failure tests cover protected exhaustion, low disk, reused directories, torn records and tampering. No synthetic result is market evidence or a fill.
