# Frozen paper execution study — 2026-10-01

This checkpoint fixes only qualification event/level/stage collapse, then freezes semantic matching. It studies monetization of `STRICT_EQUIVALENT` and `ORDINARY_EQUIVALENT_BASIS_RISK` episodes. Unresolved/rejected routes never enter profit totals. PAPER/read-only: no order, account, funding, wallet or ledger mutations. Ohio/live-launch gates remain closed. Public US market data and the existing read-only authenticated WS subscriptions are the only collection interfaces.

## Entry and evidence

Native WS books must be fresh within 2 seconds, sequence/version-valid, live, identity-bound, with proven orientation, open markets, known positive minimum quantities, known supported fees, sufficient equal integer depth and positive **normal-fee** net. Normal-fee rounding uses consumed levels: Kalshi rounds up per consumed level; PM-US aggregates and rounds ties to even. Fees remain estimates of taker execution, including unconfirmed account precision and possible within-level fragmentation. One-tick and extreme-fragmentation fees are diagnostics, never the main entry gate. Exact source levels, full signal books, proof, IDs, orientation, timestamps, ages, sizes, fees, gross/net and certificate are retained.

Five independent entry-cap scenarios: $5/$10/$25/$50/$100. $10 is the baseline; all use $100 simulated cash per venue ($200 total), **not live authorization**. The largest profitable supported integer quantity within the cap, venue minimums and displayed depth is chosen at signal time, with a finite 200-contract ceiling. Sensitivities use identical recorded books; they are alternative portfolios, not additive trades. The prior $5/$50-per-venue detector/history is preserved. No quantity or policy is selected using the final run's outcomes.

## Episodes and latency

A route plus orientation starts an episode when eligible. One episode generates one attempt per scenario. Repeated unchanged books do not generate entries. Content changes count quote updates. An observed economic absence lasting at least 1 second ends the episode at its first absent reading; a shorter flicker is merged. Missing/stale observations lasting 2 seconds censor it. Observation resumes as a censored episode, not proven economic recurrence. First-seen opportunities are left-censored; open opportunities at window end are right-censored. Duration is measured only within observed coverage. Sampling gaps and processing lag limit exact disappearance time; quote medians are content-update weighted, not time-weighted.

Kalshi is the conditional first leg at signal time. PM-US is evaluated at 0/10/25/50/100/250/500/1,000 ms against actual recorded subsequent native states. Prices are reconstructed as of the requested monotonic timestamp, using only states captured at or before it. Later prices cannot fill an earlier leg. Frames received before a target but processed afterward invalidate that target. Current feed health and freshness corroborate reconstruction; disconnection/restart, stale/missing data, backlog, unknown version or a callback more than 250 ms late produce `UNOBSERVED`. Unchanged native state can be used only while valid/fresh and continuously observed; that is a quote-survival observation, not proof liquidity remained available to our order. Callback lag and unchanged-state counts are retained separately.

The first leg's displayed take is **conditional**, not an actual fill. There is no public evidence of our order acceptance, priority or competing takers. Later depth is repriced independently and cannot be inferred from signal depth. Only positive-net second-leg quantities within the original paired cap are taken. A smaller hedge becomes `PARTIAL_HEDGE`; available but uneconomic quotes become `EDGE_DISAPPEARED`; no second depth becomes `FIRST_LEG_ONLY`. Missing evidence becomes `UNOBSERVED`. These are conditional taker-depth outcomes, an upper bound on execution feasibility; no actual fill count or realized profit is claimed.

For residual first-leg contracts, one fixed unwind diagnostic checks actual first-venue bids 250 ms after the selected second-leg timestamp, charges normal sell fees, and records `UNWIND`, `ORPHAN` or `UNOBSERVED`. Entry outcomes and terminal diagnostic outcomes remain separate. Partial residuals or unobserved unwinds are not assigned a fabricated value. This is not a recovery operating retest or a live order policy.

## Portfolio, settlement and business decision

Chronological paper portfolios allow one entry per episode and suppress any route sharing a still-held native market. This avoids counting the same liquidity across routes or opposite orientations. Reserve first-leg cost plus fees and all remaining cap on PM-US before entry; skip if cash cannot cover that reservation. Realized diagnostic unwind proceeds can return cash. Complementary positions and unknown exposure stay reserved; settlement payouts are never recycled within the window. No hindsight ranking or reallocating to the strongest later quote.

Clean/paired profit assumes ordinary complementary $1 payout per pair. Basis trades keep their native payout clauses, ordinary predicate certificate and exceptional divergence branches; no invented rare-event probability. Strict and basis counts, P&L and exposures are reported separately. Portfolio P&L/ROI is null if any accepted residual or hedge is unpriced; the known ordinary profit subtotal and unpriced exposure are shown instead. Capital lockup uses the later native close date plus a **24-hour assumed settlement buffer**; it is a proxy, not an exact contractual settlement deadline. Missing/nonfuture dates reserve indefinitely; administrative extensions can exceed this proxy. Actual settlement and repeatability over resolution cycles are unproven.

One instrumentation smoke of 60 seconds is permitted, then one frozen **45-minute** observation. Preparation is bounded at 20 minutes; supervision at 70 minutes; evidence at 1 GiB; at most 10,000 episodes / 512 simultaneous captures. No strategy tuning, tests/builds or repeated CI polling during collection. All collection stops at completion. Source and parameter hashes are verified before and after.

Descriptive decision thresholds, frozen before observation: fewer than 30 episodes is insufficient frequency; zero observable baseline delayed outcomes or instrumentation truncation blocks the execution study; promising requires at least 10 capital-admitted baseline entries, positive fully observed modeled P&L and at least 80% clean pairs across all baseline eligible episodes at 100 ms. Otherwise economics is weak. Thresholds are a checkpoint rubric, not statistical proof or a recommendation to trade. A single 45-minute window cannot establish repeatable net profit or justify live money.


## Reporting and observed limits

After collection stopped, a reporting-only correction merges freshness-separated capture segments until a witnessed economic disappearance. Those conservative economic groups are a lower bound, not inferred continuous price histories. Survival curves choose each group's first chronologically eligible $10-cap capture. Raw segment curves, all quotes and the frozen raw report remain retained. No entry/size/capital/latency policy or price changed; every sensitivity portfolio and baseline P&L is unchanged. The median entry edge counts one first-entry quote per economic group; content-update medians remain diagnostics. The paper admission policy is separate from legacy `ArbEvaluation.execution` diagnostic flags and never grants a production/live approval.

The 1 GiB guard ended the planned 45-minute observation at 42.23 minutes. Claimed consumed-level evidence is complete and independently verified; unobserved futures remain unpriced. Full opportunity duration is unknown through freshness gaps. The later native closing date plus a day is a conservative **proxy**, not observed settlement time; it may be much later than actual settlement or earlier than an administrative extension.
